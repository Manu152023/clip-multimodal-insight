Privacy Policy for CLIP MultiModal Insight

Effective Date: August 16, 2026

CLIP MultiModal Insight ("the App") is developed to provide multimodal analysis and insight from user-provided content such as images, videos, and text.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not require users to create an account. Users may voluntarily provide images, videos, text, or other content for analysis.

2. How We Use Your Information

User-provided content is used to provide the functionality of CLIP MultiModal Insight, including multimodal analysis, interpretation, summarization, and generation of insights.

3. Storage and Retention

Where processing occurs locally on your device, user content is not transmitted to our servers. Temporary processing data may remain on your device until it is removed.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information and do not share user-provided content with third parties for advertising purposes.

5. Permissions

The App may request permissions necessary for its functionality, such as access to media selected by the user. Permissions are used only for the purposes explained within the App.

6. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. No method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Children's Privacy

CLIP MultiModal Insight is not specifically directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Data Deletion

Users may stop using the App and delete locally stored application data by uninstalling the App, subject to Android's normal device behavior and backups.

9. Third-Party Services

The current version of the App does not intentionally use third-party advertising services to collect personal information for advertising purposes.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the App's functionality or data practices change. Updated policies will be published at this location.
